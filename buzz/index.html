Quick menu

Drag anywhere to steer. The lever on the right sets speed; pull it to the bottom to glide. Come in low and slow over a lit H to land; push the lever up or tap TAKE OFF to lift straight off. PULSE sets off everything close by. Tap the map to enlarge it. Double tap the screen for this menu.

Season, time, weather

Tap to change. Auto follows this phone's date and clock; auto weather is a seasonal pick, not a forecast.

Landing spots

Each spot has a lit H and a column of light above it. Green = not landed yet. First landing +500, later landings +150, and landing banks whatever Havoc you are carrying.
