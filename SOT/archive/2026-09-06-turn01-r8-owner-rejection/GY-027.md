# GY-027 — R8 hides the operating picture and treats SSOT as an overview mode

Date: 2026-09-06
Status: EMPHATIC OWNER REJECTION — UI / information architecture

## Owner evidence

The qualified R8 reconciliation backend correctly deduplicates global content, but the R8 product surface is rejected.

Observed failures:

- A project can be actively indexing while its primary card says only `Needs scan` and offers `Scan now`.
- Live work is visible only after opening Advanced / diagnostics.
- The four small SSOT summary boxes replaced the intended visual estate bars and do not communicate coverage or progress.
- The `Storage estate` button incorrectly treats the home operating picture as a mode instead of the default product home page.
- The project/detail surface wastes most of the screen and gives little actionable information.
- The product still frames projects as the dominant navigation concept instead of the SSOT database.
- There is no first-class way to inspect the underlying SSOT database/catalog directly.

## Replacement contract

The next surface is a database-centered master/detail application:

1. **Dashboard is home.** No Overview/Storage-estate button is required to return to truth.
2. **Dashboard shows visual storage bars** for unique content, verified Copy A, verified Copy B, fully protected, and unresolved/unknown content.
3. **Active work is impossible to miss.** Every running/queued/paused project appears in an Active now strip with live files/bytes/progress and appropriate Pause/Stop/Resume controls.
4. **Master/detail.** Project master rows remain on the page; selecting one renders its detail below the master without navigation/context loss.
5. **Deep dive modal.** Details opens a large modal containing sources, fingerprints/copy coverage summaries, operation history/diagnostics, and export/print/share actions.
6. **Database is first-class.** A dedicated Database surface allows search and inspection by content fingerprint, location/project membership, verified Copy A/Copy B state, and size.
7. **Activity is first-class.** Durable activity/event history is visible without exposing worker internals on the dashboard.
8. Technical lifecycle states remain secondary. The operator sees storage truth and current work first.

R8 UI is evidence only and must not be patched forward. The qualified R8 reconciliation backend may remain as the read-model foundation.
