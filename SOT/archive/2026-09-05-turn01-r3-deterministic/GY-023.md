# GY-023 — Mechanically qualified but operationally unintelligible R2 project surface

**Status:** REJECTED  
**Decision date:** 2026-09-05

## Rejected approach

Treat a project as a technically complete vertical collection of Scope/Storage, Index, Review, Plan, Execute and Activity controls without a strong operational hierarchy, while also putting lifecycle controls on the left project rail and retaining AI/provider configuration in the immediate product surface.

## Why it is rejected

The surface was mechanically qualified but failed owner product testing. It remained difficult to understand what to do after Source/Target setup and fingerprint/index. It exposed backend lifecycle machinery rather than converting deterministic evidence into a clear storage decision workflow. The project rail was not a quiet read-only status instrument, and the right side did not reliably answer the three required operator questions: which project, what is true now, and what should happen next.

## Required replacement

R3 uses basic deterministic mechanics only:

- left rail = read-only project selection + continuously current status;
- right side = selected-project operations cockpit;
- one explicit next-action strip;
- completed stages collapse to compact truth summaries;
- current stage is visually dominant;
- later stages remain visible but unavailable until their deterministic prerequisites exist;
- workflow is `Storage -> Index -> Review -> Plan -> Execute -> Protected`;
- Activity/Diagnostics is subordinate evidence, not primary navigation;
- Review translates fingerprints into storage facts: unique content, duplicate storage, copy cardinality, missing-on-Target and missing-on-Backup;
- Plan is deterministic and binds committed evidence revision;
- Execute uses only the current deterministic plan and verifies results;
- AI, Venice, OpenRouter, inference and AI POV are excluded from the R3 operational surface.

The qualified coordination backend remains accepted and unchanged.
