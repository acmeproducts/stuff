# simple.md - Master Plan

## Define
Purpose: A single-file, mobile-first HTML application that lists Yahoo News articles with headlines, summaries, and thumbnail images, providing a lightweight way to stay updated on current events.
Users: Mobile and desktop users seeking quick, visually concise access to Yahoo News headlines without leaving the browser.
Outcomes: Users can view a curated list of Yahoo News articles, click through to full articles, and experience a responsive layout that adapts to screen size.
Success Criteria:
- App loads without errors on modern mobile browsers.
- Headlines and summaries are displayed correctly.
- Layout adjusts fluidly between 320px and 1200px widths.
- No console errors during normal use.
