How to read Market Navigator

One shared horizon ribbon controls the Regime, Risk, Growth and Macro charts. The dashboard is deliberately one page: Regime + interpretation on top, the three child indexes below.

Every selected horizon establishes T0 = 50. Components are measured from T0, normalized by their model scale and direction, weighted into Risk/Growth/Macro, then averaged into Regime.

The dashed 42 and 58 lines are the neutral boundaries. The chart crossing those lines is the primary visual signal; state and direction text are secondary confirmation.
