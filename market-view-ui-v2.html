How to read Market Navigator

Market Navigator is a windshield, not a trading terminal. It shows directional market and economic conditions rather than attempting millisecond timing.

Every selected horizon establishes T0 = 50. Each component is measured from its value at T0 to the selected date, normalized by its declared scale and direction, then multiplied by its model weight. Risk, Growth and Macro are weighted child rollups. Regime is the equal-third mean of those three indexes.

Index charts show a visible neutral band from 42–58. State, direction and child contribution are separate concepts. Component tabs show model weights and contribution. Rates/yields/spreads use percentage-point change; return-like series use percent change.
