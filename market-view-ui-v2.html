Market Navigator

The dashboard answers a simple question: are investment conditions becoming more or less favorable, and which indicators are driving that change?

Every component follows one grammar: Indicator: Now · Delta / Delta%, then relative context · direction · implication. T0 remains visible at the start of the chart and in detail. Green means favorable, amber means neutral, red means unfavorable. A negative numerical delta may therefore be green when falling is economically favorable.

Family summaries are breadth indicators. They are shorthand for the underlying evidence, not scientific measurements.
