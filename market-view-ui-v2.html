How to read Market Navigator

Market Navigator is a windshield, not a trading terminal. The purpose is directional situational awareness: where the road is going, what the gauges say, and which road signs matter.

The hierarchy is literal. Regime is the persisted rollup of Risk, Growth and Macro. Each index is persisted from its declared component series. Drilling down exposes exactly the children that produced the parent.

Index charts are 0–100 and changes are shown in index points. Component charts are rebased to 0% at the selected horizon start; actual starting and ending raw values are labeled on the line. Every chart has dated X-axis labels and an interactive crosshair.
