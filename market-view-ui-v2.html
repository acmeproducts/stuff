Market Navigator Change Map

The dashboard asks: are investment conditions becoming more or less favorable, and how broadly do the indicators agree?

Every component uses one grammar: Indicator: Now   Delta / Delta%, followed by relative context · direction · implication. T0 is marked on the chart and shown in detail.

Green means favorable, amber means materially unchanged/mixed, and red means unfavorable. Color follows economic implication, never the mathematical sign. Falling inflation, WTI, VIX, claims, spreads and restrictive rates can therefore be favorable while rising SPY, QQQ and growth activity can also be favorable.

Relative context is shown as Unusually low, Below normal, Typical, Above normal, or Unusually high. The underlying percentile is kept in detail only. Family and Environment summaries are breadth measures—not synthetic 0–100 scores.
