What Market Navigator answers

Regime: How favorable is the current investment environment relative to the selected historical period, what evidence is driving it, and is that evidence strengthening or weakening?

Risk: How elevated is current market and credit stress relative to the selected period?

Growth: Is economic activity expanding, stable, slowing, or contracting?

Macro: Is inflation pressure building/easing, and are policy, rates, credit and liquidity becoming more supportive or restrictive?

The assessments are qualitative conclusions from deterministic observations, persistence, trend, confirmation and relative historical position. Raw component histories remain factual; OpenRouter explains the evidence but does not determine the classification.
