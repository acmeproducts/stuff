# Sources and licences: Khmer and Lao word lists

`km.json` (32,369 words) is built from the khmerlbdict word lists (SEAlang Khmer frequency list, Khmer Bible word lists, place, village and name lists) by SIL NRSI, MIT licence, Copyright (c) 2015 SIL NRSI: https://github.com/sbbic/khmerlbdict. Words are ordered by frequency, then the remaining lists. Zero-width characters removed; only Khmer-script words kept.

`lo.json` (30,549 words) is built from the LibreOffice `lo_LA` Lao dictionary, version 0.1, 2013, LGPL, Copyright 2013 Brian Eugene Wilson and Robert Martin Campbell: https://github.com/LibreOffice/dictionaries/tree/master/lo_LA. Affix flags removed; only Lao-script words kept.

Both are plain word lists used for typing suggestions. Nothing else from those projects is included.

# Chinese (Simplified) word lists

`zh.json` (64,911 words) and `zh-pinyin.json` (42,659 pinyin strings mapping to ranked words) are built from the jieba word-frequency dictionary (https://github.com/fxsjy/jieba, MIT licence, Copyright (c) 2013 Sun Junyi) with pinyin readings generated by pypinyin (https://github.com/mozillazg/python-pinyin, MIT licence). Only Han-script words up to 8 characters are kept; single-character readings include all common pronunciations, ranked below the main reading. Used for typing suggestions and pinyin input only.
