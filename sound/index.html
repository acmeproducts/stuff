This build runs on generated test data: 20 years of one person's days, with sleep, commutes, meetings, a louder city stretch early on, a louder-at-home stretch in the middle, one quiet year, concerts, travel, and days the phone was off. Hatching means nothing was recorded.

The calendar tiles cleanly: a month is 4 weeks, a year is 13 months (364 days), a decade is 10 of those years. Days, hours and minutes follow the real clock.
