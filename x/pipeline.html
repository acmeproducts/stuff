X 1.0 Pipeline

One pipe, six gates, eleven streams, five plans. Status as of the evening of 18 Nov 2026: X's first pass failed on your devices, and the order changed to letters, words, then showing. The full record is in the repo under x/; this page is its picture.

True north

The human talks and minimal taps or swipes X, and X talks to everything else. X is multi threaded. X eliminated the continued decimation of the human hand, wrist, and elbow because X is kind of like chocolate. Chocolate just loves you no matter what.
Owner, 16 Nov 2026
Your version of X is born, and just like a child the first thing you do is develop a vocabulary that evolves. It starts with I'm crying because I'm hungry and evolves all the way to reciting Shakespeare and arguing quantum mechanics.
Owner, 18 Nov 2026
  • X is a learning system. Born knowing five things. It grows only by addition, from your verdicts.
  • It learns in operation and in function. What it learns stays on your device and syncs to GitHub; the build reads it and X itself improves.
  • Not a dashboard, not a chat box, not a hub. One thing in front. No connectors of its own.
  • AI assists in a set order, only at the edge of what X already knows.
  • No source is required. Watch, hand and head are interchangeable; an arbiter you teach decides whose word counts.
  • What is built is the floor, not the blueprint.

The order of operations inside X

Every exchange runs these nine steps, all inside the X page. Steps 4 to 6 are one call straight to an AI model with your key, the call prism already makes. No gateway. Nothing to do with OpenClaw. A word X already knows skips step 4.

1

Sense. Voice, watch, hand or head reports.

2

Arbitrate. Whose report counts here.

3

X is born. X does something it was taught or shown, by a signal or a word of yours, without asking a model. What it learned is kept and synced. Hand time is reported.

First try (a voice page, X1) failed on 18 Nov. Reworded that evening; reached through V1, V3, V5, V6. Override me if the wording is wrong.

4

Read. What did you mean? AI

5

Gather. Fetch the material and freeze it as evidence. AI

6

Compose. Make the answer from the evidence. AI

7

Present. One thing, spoken and shown.

8

Verdict. Keep it, change it, or drop it.

9

Learn. What you keep becomes a word, saved on the device and synced to GitHub. Next time step 3 catches it.

Commands at birth: more, enough, yes, no, again. Walking adds: back, glide. Running adds: take, turn, let go.

The five plans

W · The watch

streams 1, 2

Does the watch live and die with X, at a cost you can afford?

W1 Session · still panel, Watch off from the page, sleeps by itself, "logged at", hardened pairing, buzz from the page. Built as turn07-b8; awaiting your watch. W2 Battery truth · percent an hour by link. W3 Recognition on the watch · needs your yes. W4 Buzz language · which buzzes you can tell apart, and what each means.

L · The Lab

streams 5, 10

Is each signal good enough to steer X?

L1 Results table · what the watch reported and each signal's record. Built in lab-r6; awaiting you. L2 Scored session · hit rate and wrong fires during X-like use. L3 Object in your hands · a cup, a bowl and a toy car taken, carried, turned and resized by one hand or both; a timed task. Built in lab-r7; awaiting you.

S · Sources and the arbiter

streams 6, 9

Can X work without the watch, and who wins when sources disagree?

S1 Camera probe · hand found or lost, frames a second, on each device. Built in lab-r6; the hand finder's first real run is on your phone. S2 Hand numbers · S3 Head and face · what the camera reads, as live numbers. Built in lab-r7; awaiting you. The face finder has never run on a device. S4 One event shape · every source reports the same way. S5 The arbiter · override, fallback, exclusive, taught per context.

V · Vocabulary and teaching

streams 7, 8

Can you teach X your own signals, join them into words, then show it what to do?

V1 Teach · letters. Show it three times, say its name. Right hand, left hand, head or watch. Built in lab-r7; awaiting you. V3 Assign · one action for each signal. V5 Words · signals made one after another become a new one. Built in lab-r7; awaiting you. V6 Showing · next. You do it once while X watches; an AI says what it was for; one signal does it again. First on the object. V4 Next-day test.

X · X itself

streams 3, 4, 11

Does X learn from you, with your hands at rest?

X1 Born · failed on your devices, 18 Nov: "a voice front end to AI". Parked. It returns when taught signals and shown runs give it something to do without asking a model each time. X2 Lists · a word brings many items; more, back, glide. X3 Threads · several at once; X announces the ready. After 1.0: X4 sentences and contexts · X5 makes and analyses · X6 acts for you, behind an explicit yes.
