Open Water

An endless procedural sea. Terrain, islands, waves and weather are generated as you go — the world rolls beneath you on recycled tiles, so it never ends and never repeats the same day twice.

Look: drag anywhere. Your vessel steers toward where you look.
Speed: W/S or the ▲▼ buttons. Turn: A/D nudges heading.
Zoom: scroll (in drone mode, scroll changes altitude).

Change vessel any time — a raft sits in the waves, a cruise ship rides above them. Slide the clock for sunrise and sunset; the sea glows after dark where it breaks. Save any spot and moment as a bookmark and return to it later, or share its code.

Rumor has it the sea holds a few secrets. Keep watch.
