Open Water

An endless procedural sea. Terrain, islands, waves and weather are generated as you go — the world rolls beneath you on recycled tiles, so it never ends and never repeats the same day twice.

Tap left/right of screen centre to look that way; tap above/below centre to tilt (in the drone: climb / descend — set it down gently on an island).
Hold to move forward — hold on the left or right side to curve that way while you go, so you can steer a real course. Double-tap and hold reverses. Drag to look around freely.
Keyboard: W/S speed, A/D turn. Scroll zooms (drone: altitude). All settings live behind the ⚙ button; every sighting scores on the counter.

Change vessel any time — a raft sits in the waves, a cruise ship rides above them. Or leave the boats behind: swim as a dolphin, a whale, or take to the air as a seabird and land wherever you like. Slide the clock for sunrise and sunset; the sea glows after dark where it breaks. Save any spot and moment as a bookmark and return to it later, or share its code.

Tap flotsam drifting past — driftwood, crates, coconuts — to haul it aboard for a point. Tap a bottle and you get a riddle in verse; solve it, sail where it points, and another bottle is waiting. Five of them make a trail. The chip beside the counter keeps your heading and range, and tapping it re-reads the verse.

Not every clue floats. Watch the flocks — one bird in each is carrying something, and only that one is worth catching. And if you put ashore on the right island you may find a lean-to someone lived in: a bedroll, a cold fire ring, and a journal. Read it to the end. Page sixty-seven is marked in quarters, and pressing the right three in the right order gives up the chart to the last bottle.
